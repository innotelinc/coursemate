Four things, in order

Every frame goes through the same pipeline, whether it is a quiz, a slide or an essay prompt.

1

Watch

Changed frames are classified into a screen kind — quiz, test, essay, reading — with the exact stem and options.

2

Listen

Lecture audio is chunked and transcribed, so what was said becomes evidence a draft can cite.

3

Remember

Slides, transcripts and readings settle into per-course facts and key terms you can inspect.

4

Answer

A validated card with its confidence, or a draft essay in your voice — plus a note when it is not sure.

The boundary, stated plainly

CourseMate decides and drafts. It does not submit, click, or answer on your behalf — a vision-driven clicker breaks on real course pages, and a wrong auto-submit on a graded test cannot be undone. The card is the product; the click is yours.
